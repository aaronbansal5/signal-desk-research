Research methodology

Data and lineage

Market prices, daily history, company fundamentals, estimates and news are requested server-side from configured providers. Alpha Vantage is the initial provider. ETF holdings should come from issuers and index constituents from index providers when adapters are configured. Each report stores source URLs, timestamps and whether values were reported or calculated. Missing data remains null—not zero.

Classification and routing

Known overrides distinguish CHAT as an actively managed thematic ETF and FNGU as a leveraged ETN. Provider metadata classifies other operating companies, ETFs and funds. Unsupported or ambiguous instruments receive limited coverage rather than company-style valuation.

Portfolio valuation

ETF trailing and forward P/E are derived from weighted earnings yields: portfolio earnings yield is the sum of weight × EPS/price, and P/E is its reciprocal. Negative earners and missing observations are excluded and their weight is disclosed. ETF PEG divides portfolio forward P/E by compatible expected portfolio EPS growth; constituent PEGs are never averaged.

Leveraged products

FNGU is analyzed in three layers: NYSE FANG+ constituents, 3× daily-reset mechanics, and the ETN. Multi-day returns are compounded from daily returns and compared with simple three-times cumulative underlying return. Scenarios are tactical and path-sensitive; they are not intrinsic-value estimates. Financing, fees, volatility decay, path dependency and issuer credit risk are explicit limitations.

Technicals

Indicators use adjusted or unadjusted daily closes consistently within a series. SMA20/50/100/200 are arithmetic means; EMA uses standard exponential weighting; RSI(14) uses smoothed gains and losses; ATR uses true range; annualized realized volatility uses daily log returns × √252. Support and resistance are probabilistic observations, never certainties.

Ratings and confidence

Ratings map a normalized 0–100 score to STRONG BUY (85–100), BUY (70–84), HOLD (45–69), SELL (30–44), or STRONG SELL (0–29). Stocks, ordinary funds and leveraged products use different weighted components. Confidence is separate and reflects coverage, freshness, stability and signal agreement.

Quality and limitations

Validation checks price availability, compatible units, valid earnings denominators, holdings totals, coverage and freshness. GOOD means core inputs passed; PARTIAL means useful but incomplete; DEGRADED means conclusions are materially constrained. Automated research can be delayed, providers can fail, analyst estimates change, and free data licenses may restrict public redistribution.
